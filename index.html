Consent Form / ಒಪ್ಪಿಗೆ ನಮೂನೆ

English: I, in my full senses, give my complete consent for my diagnostic examination, biopsy, transfusion or operation under any type of anaesthesia, and agree that no responsibility will be attached to the surgeon or hospital staff, and that the risk has been explained to me in my own language.
